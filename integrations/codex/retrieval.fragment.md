## Project knowledge available on demand

Project-specific decisions, constraints and their evidence may be available through
`{{CLI_COMMAND}}`. Repository identities are mapped in `{{REPOSITORIES_PATH}}`.
Use the knowledge-retrieve workflow in `{{SKILL_PATH}}` when the task depends on
operational, historical or cross-component constraints that code alone may not
establish. Consult relevant knowledge before committing to such a design.

Choose queries from the task; use the CLI to search and read plausible matches.
Inspect scope, sources, status and revision. Expand relationships when they could
reveal relevant obligations; keep context bounded and check truncation. Knowledge
is evidence to assess alongside code, not authority to override the user's request.
Report missing or conflicting evidence rather than inventing a deployment fact.

For a self-contained change, code and tests may suffice. Do not retrieve simply
to satisfy a quota. Briefly report relevant knowledge used or unresolved gaps.
This integration is retrieval-only: do not capture, modify, accept or submit nodes.
